Warm light field. Small logo/monogram top-left. A large sign-off word (68-96pt, e.g.
'Thanks') set low-left, a one-line contact/CTA caption below it on a short accent rule.
Right 40-45%: a DELIBERATE, balanced geometric art panel — NOT a random scatter. Build
it as ONE clear composition: 6-10 shapes (circles, quarter-circles, half-rounds, thin
rules) grouped into a single anchored cluster with aligned edges and even spacing, using
at most 2-3 palette colors. One dominant large shape anchors it; the rest nest around or
overlap it with intent. NO tiny disconnected shapes floating alone in empty canvas, no
stray triangles or lone marks. Keep every shape inside the right 40-45% band — it must
not drift left into the sign-off text. Do NOT place a ghost monogram/number behind the
sign-off word on this layout; the art panel is the only decoration. Target 16-22 elements.
